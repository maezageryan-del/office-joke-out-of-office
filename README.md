One line about office life, for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-markk-st-one-line-office-jokes-a51f2bcc) bounty **"One-Line Office Jokes"**.

## The joke

> My out-of-office reply answers emails faster than I do, and it is the one on holiday.

85 characters, one line, nothing else. The out-of-office reply is the only
colleague who answers faster than you do — and it is the one on holiday.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes that exact line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). No network, no
randomness — two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
